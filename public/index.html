Youth Leaders Programme

Business China YLP Immersion Programme - Shenzhen

Programme Duration:

  • 2 Pre-trip workshops (half-day) on 7th and 14th November
  • 7 days' immersion in Shenzhen

Who Should Attend?

  • Young entrepreneurs and next-generation business leaders
  • Corporate executives and management professionals
  • Leaders responsible for innovation, strategy or digital transformation
  • Professionals seeking business opportunities in China and the Greater Bay Area
  • Participants interested in AI, robotics, healthcare technology and smart manufacturing

Key Takeaways

By the end of the programme, participants will gain:

  • A deeper understanding of Shenzhen and the Greater Bay Area as one of China’s most dynamic innovation and business ecosystems
  • First-hand insights into China’s AI and technology development, including robotics, digital platforms, healthcare technology and smart manufacturing
  • Practical perspectives from leading companies and innovators on how technology is being applied to real business challenges
  • A better understanding of how Chinese companies innovate, scale and compete in fast-changing domestic and global markets
  • New connections and business perspectives through engagement with entrepreneurs, executives and industry leaders in China
